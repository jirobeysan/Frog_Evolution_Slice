# Frog Evolution Slice

A tiny game made for [Game Off 2026](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2026) (theme: **SHIFT**).

Eat, grow, SHIFT form. You are a small frog in a pond-and-grassland arena. Eat 30 insects to evolve twice — leg power, then a long tongue, then slimy skin — and reach the top of the pond.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (PC or mobile). No build step, no external assets — a single HTML file that also works over `file://`.

- **Move:** WASD / arrow keys, or tap and drag on touch
- **Eat:** by touching prey, or with the long tongue after the first evolution
- One playthrough takes about 2–3 minutes

## Rules

- Theme SHIFT, read as form shift: eating changes your body and what you can do.
- Two snakes patrol the arena. Before your final form, getting caught resets the prey you ate in that stage. In your final (slimy) form, snakes can't catch you — they just knock you back.
- The arena wraps around at the edges, and so does your tongue: a long tongue can reach prey across the edge.
